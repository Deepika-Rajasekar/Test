Retail Analytics Case Study

RETAIL SALES PERFORMANCE ANALYSIS

PythonPandas SQLPower BI
Problem
Retail chain facing 12% revenue decline with no visibility into root cause by product, region, or segment
Dataset
Public retail sales dataset — 80K+ transactions, 12 months, multi-region with SKU-level granularity
Analysis
SQL aggregations for region/SKU performance; Python EDA for seasonality; Power BI dashboard for exec summary
Insight
Top 20% of SKUs drove 78% of revenue; bottom 40% were margin-negative; Region 3 had low conversion despite high footfall
Impact
Inventory reallocation + staffing restructure projected to recover 18% margin gap
Business Impact: Identified ₹2.4M revenue leakage traceable to 3 under-optimised regions. Inventory consolidation recommendation modelled an 18% margin improvement without additional spend.
E-Commerce · Customer Intelligence Case Study

CUSTOMER CHURN PREDICTION & SEGMENTATION

PythonScikit-learn TableauExcel
Problem
E-commerce platform losing 25% of subscribers annually with no early-warning system or retention strategy
Dataset
Public e-commerce dataset — 50K customers, purchase history, support logs, login frequency over 18 months
Analysis
RFM segmentation in Python; logistic regression churn model; Tableau dashboard for retention team
Insight
45+ day inactivity = 74% churn probability; high-value churners had 2× more unresolved tickets — service was root cause
Impact
Early-warning model enables targeted outreach — modelled ₹4.2L retention uplift annually
Business Impact: RFM model identified 4 customer clusters; top cluster had 8× higher LTV. Churn model provided 30-day advance warning, enabling targeted re-engagement before cancellation.
Healthcare · Operational Efficiency Case Study

HOSPITAL READMISSION ANALYSIS

SQLExcelPower BI
Problem
Hospital network faces rising 30-day readmissions triggering regulatory penalties and hitting patient satisfaction scores
Dataset
Public hospital admissions dataset — 35K records, diagnosis codes, discharge day, ward, comorbidity flags
Analysis
SQL cohort analysis by discharge day, ward, and comorbidity count; Power BI heatmap for clinical leadership
Insight
Friday discharges = 31% higher readmission; patients with 3+ comorbidities had 2.8× risk; Cardiology outlier identified
Impact
Protocol redesign recommendation projected to save $340K/year in penalty costs
Business Impact: Identified 3 high-leverage protocol changes that, if implemented, model a 22% reduction in preventable readmissions and recovery of $340K in annual regulatory penalties.
Marketing Analytics · D2C Case Study

DIGITAL MARKETING CAMPAIGN ROI ANALYSIS

PythonSeaborn TableauGoogle Sheets
Problem
D2C brand spending equally across 5 channels with no visibility into which convert to actual revenue
Dataset
Simulated marketing dataset — 10K sessions/day, 5 channels, 6-month campaign window with conversion events
Analysis
Python ROAS calculation per channel; funnel drop-off analysis in Seaborn; attribution comparison vs last-click
Insight
Google Search drove 45% of revenue on 18% of budget; Email had 9× ROI vs paid social for existing customers
Impact
Budget reallocation modelled a 34% overall ROI increase with zero additional spend
Business Impact: Revealed a systematic budget misallocation — Instagram received 3× its optimal share. Reallocation model projected 34% ROI improvement without increasing total marketing spend.
Supply Chain · Logistics Case Study

SUPPLY CHAIN DELAY ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS

SQLPythonPower BI
Problem
Logistics company averaging 3.4-day SLA breaches, triggering escalations and contract penalty clauses
Dataset
Simulated logistics dataset — 14 hubs, 100K shipments, delay timestamps, supplier categories, seasonal flags
Analysis
SQL root cause query by hub, season, and supplier; Python heatmap for delay pattern; Power BI ops dashboard
Insight
68% of all delays traced to 3 of 14 hubs; Oct–Dec delays 4× higher — no seasonal capacity plan; Supplier B had 2× lead time
Impact
Targeted hub fixes modelled a 58% SLA breach reduction, recovering ₹8L in penalties
Business Impact: By fixing just 3 underperforming hubs, the model projects a 58% reduction in SLA breaches — recovering ₹8L in annual penalty fees without adding new infrastructure.
